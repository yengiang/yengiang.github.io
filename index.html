Project 1: CosMx vs. Xenium Comparative Analysis

  • Compared the performance of the 1000-plex CosMx Mouse Neuro Panel to the 247-plex Xenium Mouse Neuro Panel using R on a high-performance computing (HPC) cluster.
  • Annotated cell types in both datasets using supervised cell typing with the InSituType package, presenting results through flightpath plots, UMAP, and heatmaps.
  • Conducted differential expression analysis of CosMx and Xenium through Seurat and MAST and visualized comparative results through volcano and violin plots.

Project 2: VAI Brain Bank Analysis

  • Analyzed quantitative pathology data from approximately 140 postmortem human brains in the VAI Brain Bank, focusing on hyperphosphorylated tau, amyloid beta (Aβ), α-synuclein, and TDP-43 accumulation across multiple brain regions using Python.
  • Developed a pseudo-longitudinal staging framework using dose-response curve modeling to align patients along disease progression trajectories.
  • Performed dimensionality reduction and disease subtyping through principal component analysis (PCA), identifying distinct pathological groupings and patterns of neurodegenerative progression.
  • Applied the Subtype and Stage Inference (SuStaIn) framework to model heterogeneous disease subtypes and progression stages from cross-sectional neuropathology data.
